At the bottom centre of the landing page there is a floating "Ask ReconPilot" button. Click it.
Verify a chat panel opens with the heading "ReconPilot assistant" and a message box labelled "Message ReconPilot".
Verify the panel shows suggested questions, including "What does Amount mismatch mean?".
Click the suggestion "What does Amount mismatch mean?". Wait up to 30 seconds for the reply to finish.
Verify the assistant replies with an explanation that mentions the amount or the difference between the bank and the ERP, and that no error message is shown.
Press Escape. Verify the panel closes and the "Ask ReconPilot" button is visible again.
